## Fact sourcing

Rule applied: only figures that could be read from a primary or dated trade
source are published; anything else is named without numbers or left out.
Every page carries a "checked on 24 September 2026" note.

- Sainsbury's High Protein / Small but Mighty: Sainsbury's press release,
  30 January 2026 (figures are launch figures).
- Sainsbury's Be Good To Yourself and branded meals: product names and pack
  sizes from Sainsbury's own product URLs. Sainsbury's blocks automated reads,
  so no calories or protein are quoted for these.
- Tesco Calorie Controlled: product names and pack sizes from Tesco product
  URLs; only the two calorie figures that matched across two separate searches
  are quoted (Chicken Roast Dinner 252 kcal, Chicken in Mushroom Sauce 269 kcal).
  Tesco's April 2026 high-protein ready meals are mentioned but not tabulated,
  because the press release could not be read.
- Asda protein power pots: Grocery Gazette, 8 January 2026 (four varieties,
  £2.50 per 250g, at least 80g fruit or veg). Per-pot protein not quoted.
- Aldi: Aldi UK product pages (per-100g nutrition, price, pack size). Per-pack
  figures are worked out from per-100g and labelled as such. US Aldi products
  (Simply Nature, Bakers Life) appeared in search and were excluded.
- Lidl Chef Select High Protein meals: Lidl GB product pages (names, 380g;
  price in store, no online nutrition). The XL line's page returns 404, so its
  "100g+ protein" figure (only seen on social media) is not published.
- Lidl High Protein meat range: Grocery Trader, 16 September 2026 (in stores
  18 September; prices).
- Label thresholds: NHS "how to read food labels" (per 100g).
- Blenders: specs from each Amazon UK listing, matched by title and ASIN via
  search on 24 September 2026. The guide states the products were not tested.
