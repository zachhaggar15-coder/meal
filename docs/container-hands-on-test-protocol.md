# Container hands-on test protocol

The buying guide may only say a container was tested when it was, by us, in the
way described here (see `docs/search-quality-rules.md`). This protocol produces
the evidence and photographs needed to rewrite the three picks honestly.

Products: the three buying-guide picks in `src/data/containerBlogPosts.js`
(`budget-compartment-50-pack`, `harbour-housewares-glass-5-pack`,
`borohouse-10-pack-glass`). Test two containers from each set.

Record the purchase date, price paid and retailer. Photograph every step on a
plain background with a phone, in daylight, and keep the originals.

| # | Test | Method | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Real capacity | Fill to the brim with water from a measuring jug | ml measured vs ml claimed |
| 2 | Portion fit | Pack one 500 kcal chicken, rice and veg portion | Fits / tight / does not close |
| 3 | Leak, upright | 100 ml of water, lid on, stand for 10 minutes on kitchen roll | Dry / damp / wet |
| 4 | Leak, in a bag | Same container on its side in a bag, 30 minute walk | Dry / damp / wet |
| 5 | Microwave | Reheat a portion to piping hot, per the listing's instructions | Warping, lid fit after, hot spots |
| 6 | Stain | Store tomato chilli 3 days, then dishwasher once | Photo before and after |
| 7 | Freezer | Freeze a filled container 48 hours, thaw in fridge | Cracks, lid fit, frost inside |
| 8 | Dishwasher | 10 normal cycles, lid and base | Warping, clouding, seal still tight (repeat test 3) |
| 9 | Stacking | Stack all containers from the set, lids on | Photo; stable / slides |

Results template (one per product):

```
product_id:
bought: YYYY-MM-DD, £__, retailer
capacity: ___ ml measured (___ ml claimed)
portion_fit:
leak_upright:
leak_bag:
microwave:
stain:
freezer:
dishwasher_10_cycles:
stacking:
verdict (one sentence):
photos: file names
```

When the results are in, the picks, the "3 Practical Picks" wording and the
product cards can be rewritten from them, and the photos replace the text-only
cards that followed the removal of Amazon images on 16 August.
