# Cost methodology

Last reviewed: 29 July 2026

## What the figures mean

MealPrep.org.uk shows planning ranges, not live basket quotations. Generated plans
use four one-person weekly tiers:

| Tier | Displayed estimate |
|---|---:|
| Very cheap | £20–£30 |
| Budget | £30–£40 |
| Moderate | £40–£55 |
| Flexible | £50–£70 |

The tier is selected by the plan seed and scales linearly when a household view
adds portions. The figure assumes a seven-day shop using common UK own-brand
ingredients. It does not add a separate price for salt, water or negligible
seasoning and does not subtract food already in the cupboard.

Legacy editorial plans can carry a separately reviewed supermarket-specific
range and, where present, a price-check date. Those values must be described as
estimates and must not be silently merged with the generated tier model.

## What the figures do not claim

- They are not connected to live retailer APIs.
- They do not guarantee a checkout total or include delivery and membership fees.
- They do not guarantee stock, promotions, loyalty prices or a specific pack size.
- They do not infer exact waste, pantry inventory or regional price differences.
- An affiliate product's displayed price band is a comparison target; users are
  directed to check the current retailer price.

## Content comparisons

Editorial cost-per-protein and meal-prep-versus-convenience comparisons use
representative UK price ranges and a stated serving or protein basis. A comparison
must show the denominator, distinguish pack price from unit value, state that
prices change and avoid presenting a range as a live quote.

## Maintenance rule

Whenever a tier or editorial range changes, review every visible copy, FAQ, email
and structured-data consumer. Exact price, offer, availability and aggregate
rating properties are not emitted in Product schema because the site does not
maintain reliable live values for them.
