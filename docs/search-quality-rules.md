# Search-quality rules

Last reviewed: 29 July 2026

## Purpose

Index a page because it is useful for a distinct search task, not because another
keyword combination can be generated.

## Required for every canonical page

- HTTP 200, self-canonical, indexable and present in exactly the intended sitemap.
- One accurate H1, a unique title and a unique description.
- Meaningful prerendered main content on mobile and desktop.
- No material factual mismatch between metadata, visible copy, seed and
  structured data.
- A defensible incoming contextual link and useful next action.
- No broken internal link, redirecting internal link or broken local asset.
- No unsupported medical, personal-testing, price, availability, user-count or
  comparative-superiority claim.

## Metadata

Lead with the query-defining attributes a person needs to choose the result.
Do not enumerate every plan dimension. Titles outside 28–70 characters and
descriptions outside 90–160 characters are reviewed, but length alone does not
justify rewriting a stable snippet. Calorie and named-supermarket claims are
blocking factual checks.

## Programmatic content

Use the eligibility policy in `docs/programmatic-page-eligibility.md`. Never add
generic filler to alter a duplicate score. Do not create a page merely because a
keyword exists. Similar pages are reviewed with visible differences, internal
journey value, Search Console evidence and engagement data.

## Content and commercial integrity

Advice must answer the query early, expose assumptions and limitations, and lead
to a practical next step. Affiliate recommendations require disclosure and honest
trade-offs. Do not fabricate testing, ratings, prices or urgency. MealPrep+ copy
must describe a coming-soon waitlist, not a live subscription.

## Change policy

Use the weekly analytics report to prioritise qualified impressions, CTR and
useful engagement. Observe the 28-day snippet-edit cooldown unless a claim is
wrong or a page is broken. Do not edit a high-impression route solely to produce
activity. All changes must preserve the release gates and receive a live canonical
crawl after production deployment.
